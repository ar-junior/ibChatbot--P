Internal Knowledge Base – GreenLeaf Office Campus

GreenLeaf Office Campus was established in March 2025 as a pilot smart office located in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. The campus consists of three buildings: Maple Block, Cedar Block, and Oak Block.

Building Details:
- Maple Block houses the Engineering Department.
- Cedar Block houses Human Resources and Finance.
- Oak Block contains Research Labs and the Innovation Center.

Office Timings:
- Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- Saturday: 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed

Parking Rules:
- Basement A is reserved for employees.
- Basement B is reserved for visitors.
- Electric vehicle charging stations are available only in Basement A.

IT Helpdesk:
- Extension: 2201
- Email: helpdesk@greenleaf.internal
- Working Hours: 8:30 AM to 7:00 PM

Meeting Rooms:
- Alpha Room: Capacity 8
- Beta Room: Capacity 15
- Gamma Room: Capacity 25

Cafeteria:
- Breakfast: 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM
- Lunch: 12:30 PM – 2:30 PM
- Snacks: 4:30 PM – 5:30 PM

Emergency Procedures:
- Fire assembly point is located near the north parking gate.
- Medical room is located on the ground floor of Cedar Block.
- Security control room extension is 999.

Employee Policies:
- Employees must carry their ID cards at all times.
- Visitors must register at the reception.
- Remote work is allowed for a maximum of two days per week with manager approval.

Library:
- Located on the second floor of Maple Block.
- Open from 9:30 AM to 5:30 PM.
- Maximum of three books can be borrowed for 14 days.

Innovation Center:
- Open only to Research Department employees.
- Access requires Level-3 authorization.
- Equipment booking must be completed at least one day in advance.
